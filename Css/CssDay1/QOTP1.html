Kim Ji-won

Queen of Tears

Kim Ji-won is a South Korean actress who plays the lead role in the drama Queen of Tears. She is admired for her expressive acting and strong screen presence.

About

In the series, her character faces challenges of love, family, and identity while showing resilience and grace.

Notable Quotes
